The one raised block on a plain page: rows on `secondary-background` with the group radius, under an optional list header.

Pass `header` for the footnote line above it ("Read as 3 entries") and `Row`s or `Field`s as children. Notes shows the structured preview of a note in it. Weight shows today's weight and body fat as two `Field`s in it while the day has none, with a prominent Save under it, so the morning's entry is the first thing on the page; once the day has a weight, the block is gone and the day is a row of the list. Nothing else on a plain page is raised.

- Do: keep one `Inset` on a page at a time, right where the thing it previews or asks for is entered.
- Don't: use it as a card for content that is not rows or fields, or on a grouped page (that is `Group`).
