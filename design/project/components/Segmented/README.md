The segmented control that chooses the period of the Weight graph: W, M, 6M, Y, as in Health.

Pass `items` (key and label), the selected `value` and `onChange`. It fills the width of its container. The track is `tertiary-fill` with `radius-control`; the selected segment is `segment-selected` with `shadow-segment`, the one shadow in the system. Labels are `footnote` weight in `label`, semibold when selected.

- Do: keep the segments to short, fixed labels, and put the control on the first line under the entry row.
- Don't: use it to switch between views; a second list lives behind a `tint` row such as Show all entries.
