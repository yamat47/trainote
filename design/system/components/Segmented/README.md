The segmented control that switches Weight between its list and its graph.

Pass `items` (key and label), the selected `value` and `onChange`. The track is `tertiary-fill` with `radius-control`; the selected segment is `segment-selected` with `shadow-segment`, the one shadow in the system. Labels are `footnote` weight in `label`, semibold when selected.

- Do: use two or three short segments, and put the control on the first line under the title.
- Don't: use it for filters with more than three values; those are pills or a menu.
