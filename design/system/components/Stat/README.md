A big number with its unit and label: the weekly tally at the top of Notes and today's weight at the top of Weight.

Pass `value`, an optional `unit` and the `label`. The value is `large-title` with tabular figures in `label`, the unit `headline` in `secondary-label` beside it, the label `footnote` in `secondary-label` beneath. Stats sit in a row with `space-4` between them.

- Do: keep to one to three stats per screen, the most important first.
- Don't: add an arrow, a colour for up or down, or a sparkline; the trend lives in the graph.
