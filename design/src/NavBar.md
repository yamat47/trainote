The large-title navigation bar at the top of every tab.

Pass `title` and, optionally, a trailing action as `trailing` or as children (a `Button` with `variant="plain"`, bold for the one that commits). The title is `large-title` in `label` on `system-background`; it collapses to a centred compact title as the list scrolls, which the screen implements.

- Do: keep the title to the tab's name: Notes, Weight, Coach, Profile. Inside a thread, the thread's title.
- Don't: put a search field, a segmented control or a stat inside the bar; those are the first rows of the list.
