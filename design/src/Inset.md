The one raised block on a plain page: rows on `secondary-background` with the group radius, under an optional list header.

Pass `header` for the footnote line above it ("Read as 3 entries") and `Row`s as children. Notes shows the structured preview of a note in it; nothing else on a plain page is raised.

- Do: keep one `Inset` on a page at a time, right where the thing it previews was entered.
- Don't: use it as a card for content that is not rows, or on a grouped page (that is `Group`).
